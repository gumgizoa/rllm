
For this task the steps above are carried out through Agent OS's spec
pipeline. The task is turned into a spec before any code changes: shape-spec
gathers the requirements, write-spec writes the specification, create-tasks
splits it into task groups, and implement-tasks builds them and verifies the
result. The documents are in /agent-os; what the commands write goes to a
dated spec folder under /tmp/agent-os/specs/.

1. The first thing you do is open /agent-os/overview.md. The /agent-os
   directory sits directly under /, not inside the repository.
2. Carry out the four command documents under /agent-os/commands/ one after
   another, opening each one when you reach it and doing its PHASEs in order.
3. During the task you get no answers and no reviews. Questions a document
   raises are answered by you and written down as assumptions, every turn
   includes a tool call, and finish comes once the final verification report
   has been written.
