
For this task the steps above are carried out with a written task list from
Agent OS. Before any code changes, create-tasks splits the change into task
groups; implement-tasks then builds them and verifies the result. The
documents are in /agent-os; what the commands write goes to a dated spec
folder under /tmp/agent-os/specs/.

1. The first thing you do is open /agent-os/overview.md. The /agent-os
   directory sits directly under /, not inside the repository.
2. Carry out the two command documents under /agent-os/commands/ one after
   the other, opening each one when you reach it and doing its PHASEs in
   order.
3. During the task you get no answers and no reviews. Every turn includes a
   tool call, and finish comes once the final verification report has been
   written.
