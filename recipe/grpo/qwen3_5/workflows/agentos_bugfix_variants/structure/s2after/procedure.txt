
For this task the steps above are followed by Agent OS's written record of
the change. Once the change is made and tested, shape-spec writes down the
requirements, write-spec the specification and create-tasks the task list of
that change, and implement-tasks checks the work against the list and
verifies the result. The documents are in /agent-os; what the commands write
goes to a dated spec folder under /tmp/agent-os/specs/.

1. The first thing you do is open /agent-os/overview.md. The /agent-os
   directory sits directly under /, not inside the repository.
2. Work the task the way the steps above describe. When the change is made
   and tested, carry out the four command documents under /agent-os/commands/
   one after another, opening each one when you reach it and doing its PHASEs
   in order.
3. During the task you get no answers and no reviews. Questions a document
   raises are answered by you and written down as assumptions, every turn
   includes a tool call, and finish comes once the final verification report
   has been written.
